# Official media and reuse
All listed image and brochure URLs point to pages/files hosted on manufacturer or brand domains where identified. That establishes source provenance, not permission to download, rehost, crop, edit, redistribute, or use in commercial content.
Before using an asset on BikeChala:
- Review the OEM site's terms of use and copyright/licensing statement.
- Prefer an OEM press/media kit or written permission for commercial reuse.
- Keep source URL, date retrieved, filename, hash and licence/permission evidence with each local asset.
- Do not use third-party dealer/marketplace photos as official OEM assets.
